The maintainer has confirmed permission to redistribute these excerpts with
Clicky, including its public repository, application downloads, and website

holders. Separate reuse and relicensing rights were not specified and are not

The full source video and audio are not included in the repository or releases.

hash, selected intervals, processing, and output hashes.
[`Assets/profiles.json`](Assets/profiles.json) maps the excerpts to runtime
profiles. The website previews use the same prepared WAV files.

## Original effects and development tools

The Soft, Crisp, Hard, Ding, and Typewriter effects are generated by
[`scripts/extract_sounds.py`](scripts/extract_sounds.py) from seeded noise and
oscillators. They are original Clicky assets covered by the MIT license.

FFmpeg is used only when regenerating the sound assets and is not bundled with
the app. A developer who installs FFmpeg receives it under that distribution's
own license. Clicky's app has no third-party runtime package dependencies;
it uses Apple's system frameworks and the local ClickyCore package.
